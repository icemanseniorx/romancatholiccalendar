About the calendar

The supplied printed calendar is the authority for feast titles, Sundays, ranks, vigils, octaves, fasts, and abstinence. Handwritten annotations are not included.

New: brief Lives of the Saints notes are included for saints and feasts that match the calendar date, rebuilt from the supplied scanned edition of Lives of the Saints (compiled from Rev. Alban Butler).

Selected historical and devotional notes are drawn only from the supplied Father Lasance Missal, The Raccolta, Pilgrim Walks in Rome, and Lives of the Saints. First Fridays and First Saturdays are also identified; alerts remain limited to fast and abstinence days.
